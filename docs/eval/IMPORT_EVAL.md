# Import eval (Phase 9 I1, deterministic tier D)

Measured 2026-11-02 on WSL (Swift 6.4, Linux) by `ImportEvalTests/metrics`. Re-run:

```
bash scripts/core-test.sh --filter ImportEvalTests                    # prints the tables below
IMPORT_EVAL_REPORT=/tmp/eval.md bash scripts/core-test.sh --filter ImportEvalTests/metrics   # also writes them
IMPORT_EVAL_DEBUG=sk-asc-human-lines bash scripts/core-test.sh --filter ImportEvalTests/metrics  # one fixture in detail
```

## What this measures, and what it does not

- **Input is synthetic.** The 39 fixtures in `Packages/TimetableCore/Tests/Fixtures/import/` are generated by
  `scripts/import-fixtures.py` from hand-written ground truth. Each one is rendered as a `RecognizedTable` the way the
  Vision adapter should deliver it: table cells (30 fixtures) or loose OCR lines with boxes (9 fixtures), with injected
  noise (look-alike characters, missing diacritics, low confidence, an unreadable day label).
- **No real photo has been through Vision yet.** These numbers cover the parser (tier D), not OCR. Vision's table
  detection, its Slovak/Czech/Polish/Hungarian/Romanian/Serbian diacritics, skew and lighting must be measured on the
  iPhone (stage I2; AI_IMPORT "Findings"). Expect real-world numbers to be lower.
- The human's aSc timetable is reproduced from the description in the Phase 9 brief. Positions the brief did not
  give were chosen: Tue SPAJ period 1, SPAJ 5, TPVAJ1 10; Wed ZaExk 3.
- Tier A (Foundation Models) is not measured here. It runs only on the device, only for low-confidence cells and only
  in supported languages (not Slovak).

## Where it fails

| Fixture | Input | Result | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| sk-asc-human-lines | loose lines | 12 / 13 | "prax" covers periods 2–7. As loose lines the short text sits over periods 4–5, so it is read as periods 4–5. **Not flagged.** |
| sk-asc-human-oneline | loose lines | 12 / 13 | The same "prax" span. |
| sk-merged-lines | loose lines | 2 / 5 | Spans of 3–4 periods with short or centred text land on the middle periods. **Not flagged.** |
| sk-asc-human-noisy | cells | 10 / 13 | "lS1" (l for 1 next to a letter) and "H4PI" are not corrected; "Muzejna" lacks its diacritic. All three are flagged (OCR confidence 0.41–0.55). |
| sk-low-confidence | cells | 3 / 5 | "S JL" and "lNF" stay as read. Both are flagged. "2O4" and "1I3" are corrected to 204 and 113, with a question. |

**All 5 silent errors are merged-cell spans in the loose-line fallback.** The line clusterer only sees text, not cell
borders, so a short label centred over six periods gives no clue to its width. Vision's table cells carry the real
span, and with them every span was right (30 / 30 cell fixtures). Mitigation: the review screen shows the photo beside
the grid, and I2 should show spans clearly. Better fix (later): read cell borders from the image.

The other wrong fields are flagged. Precision is 45 %: most "flagged but correct" lessons are corrected look-alikes
("2O4" → 204). Each of these also gets a question, which is the intended behaviour.

## Per fixture

`ImportEvalTests/metrics` prints the full per-fixture table (structure checks, lessons right / expected, spurious,
wrong but not flagged). On 2026-11-02 every fixture not listed under "Where it fails" was fully correct. That includes
the transposed aSc table, A/B weeks (cell markers and repeated day runs), 90-minute university blocks (printed, and
inferred from start times only), a zero period, a missing header row (times assumed, `.timesAssumed` notice), an
unreadable day label (inferred from the order and flagged `.unknownDay`), a title row, two header rows, a hidden
teacher field (moved to notes), teacher titles (Mgr., RNDr., PhD. after the name, 先生) and day names in every app
language, with and without diacritics.

## Guards in CI

`ImportEvalTests` fails if:
- there are fewer than 30 fixtures or any of the 12 languages is missing;
- a lesson's text is not an input cell, or a subject is not in its cell (lessons are never invented);
- parsing is not deterministic;
- a candidate does not become a valid `SharePayloadV1`;
- structure-exact drops below 90 %, subject accuracy below 90 %, or recall below 40 %.

These limits sit just under today's numbers, so they catch regressions. They are not targets.
